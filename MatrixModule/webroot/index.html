Phase 4 Security Policy & Architecture

Security Boundaries Enforced:

  • No Hardware Attestation Forgery: Does not inject into Keystore HAL or spoof TEE certificates.
  • No Keybox Integration: Never extracts, stores, or utilizes unauthorized private key material.
  • No Play Integrity Bypass: Does not falsify DroidGuard verdicts or DroidGuard tokens.
  • No Package Cloaking: Does not hook system_server or PackageManager to deceive banking applications.
  • Non-Destructive Coexistence: Never automatically removes or disables third-party modules.
